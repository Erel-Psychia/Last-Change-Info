Wheels of Prio; V5.7; https://psyra-productions.itch.io/wheels-of-prio;
Added music for each race track.
Added a progress sound that indicates the percentage of your car health. For example, if your maximum car health is 200 and your current health is 100, a progress bar at 50 percent pitch will play every time your car is hit without a shield.
The health progress sound can now be toggled in gameplay settings.
Updated the song that plays when you win the race. It is the same song, but with better instruments.
Different songs will now play, depending if you have won or lost the race.
Added an option to randomly choose a race track.
Added coin sounds that play while counting points. At first, the volume of the coin sounds are consistent. Since counting points will speed up the counter, the volume of coin sounds will be more randomized to avoid causing noise.
Added the ability to distribute points to defense, health and speed. Distributing requires at least 3 points. The remainder is stored for later use.
The number of points can now be viewed in car stats.
Added an option to lock the current difficulty, preventing it from increasing when you win a race.
Fixed some small errors found in the points assignment menu.
Added a few extra sounds for the speed boost.
Automatic cars will now slow down when the race is over. Additionally, when watch after victory is turned off, the player's car will become automatic when the race is over.
Added sounds that play when difficulty level is increased.
